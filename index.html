How it works

AI-accelerated processing, backed by dedicated AP specialists.

Pure software leaves your team stuck on messy exceptions. Pure manual entry slows your month-end close. We pair AI data extraction with your own dedicated AP team.

The process

How every invoice is processed, in 4 stages

1

Capture & extract

AI reads invoices from email, PDFs, scanned paper and EDI — vendor name, invoice number, GSTIN/tax ID, due date and every line item.

2

Code & validate tax

GL codes and cost centres are predicted from your history. GSTIN, HSN/SAC and e-invoice QR codes (or W-9/VAT) are checked automatically.

3

Match 2-way / 3-way

Invoice lines are checked against POs and GRNs in your ERP. Clean matches go for approval; variances and duplicates are held for review.

4

Specialist review

When the AI isn't sure — a blurry scan or complex terms — your dedicated AP specialist steps in, resolves it and helps the AI improve.
